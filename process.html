Our process

How we work

From the first meeting to the final file, here’s exactly what working with us looks like. It comes down to three clear steps.

01 · Pre-shoot02 · Shoot day03 · Post-shoot
  1. 01

    Step 1Pre-shoot

    Planning the story

    We sit down with you for a planning meeting and map out how your video, or each video, will look, from the storyline through to the final shot.

    • Planning meeting
    • Storyline for every video
    • Know exactly what you’re getting
  2. 02

    Step 2Shoot day

    On the day

    We run through the plan once more so everything is clear and agreed. Then we get to work and film it.

    • Quick run-through of the plan
    • Any last changes sorted on the spot
    • Filming and photography
  3. 03

    Step 3Post-shoot

    Editing and delivery

    We edit, colour grade and caption your content, then deliver it within 1–5 days. One revision session is included, so it lands exactly right.

    • Edited, colour graded and captioned
    • 1–5 day delivery
    • 1 revision session included

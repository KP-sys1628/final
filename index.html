Our story

Where it all began

KiwoProductions began as a dream in 2022, built on a simple but meaningful vision: to bring people’s ideas, visions and dreams to life through cinematics.

What started as a passion for creative expression grew into something much bigger — a desire to help people feel confident in how they present themselves, express their individuality and share their brand with the world.

More about us

Our mission

“Our mission at KiwoProductions is to capture a feeling, tell a story and create something that truly represents the person or vision behind it.”

Being able to turn someone’s ideas into reality, knowing that our work has helped them feel confident, inspired and satisfied, is what makes what we do so rewarding.

At KiwoProductions, creativity is at the heart of everything we do. We’re here to bring visions to life, create meaningful experiences and help others see their ideas become something real.

Our process

How we work

From the first meeting to your finished content, in three clear steps.

See our full process
01Step 1 · Pre-shoot

Planning the story

We sit down with you for a planning meeting and map out how your video, or each video, will look, from the storyline through to the final shot.

02Step 2 · Shoot day

On the day

We run through the plan once more so everything is clear and agreed. Then we get to work and film it.

03Step 3 · Post-shoot

Editing and delivery

We edit, colour grade and caption your content, then deliver it within 1–5 days. One revision session is included, so it lands exactly right.

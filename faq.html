FAQ

Commonquestions

Everything you need to know about working with us. Can’t find it here? Just ask.

What services do you offer?

Videography, photography and advertisement. We don’t specialise in one niche: short-form for TikTok, Reels and Shorts, long-form YouTube content, brand films, events, portraits, product shots and ad creative for businesses of every kind and size.

See our services
How much does it cost?

10 Edited Stills: $99.99. Short-Form Video: $149.99. Long-Form Video: from $499.99. Bundles: $199.99 to $849.99 (Starter Pack, Content Drop, Creator Pack, Brand Day Package). All prices are in Australian dollars (AUD) and include editing.

See the full price chart
Why is long-form video priced “from” $499.99?

Long-form (YouTube) content starts at a $499.99 base rate. The final price depends on things like length, locations, crew and any extra requirements, so we quote each one individually.

Get a quote
Which package is right for me?

Starter Pack ($199.99): One short-form video and a set of photos, filmed and edited. Content Drop ($399.99): A quick, sharp batch of social content from a single session. Creator Pack ($599.99): The full shoot experience. Video and photo in one session, enough content to last weeks. Brand Day Package ($849.99): Full production session. Multiple looks, locations, or a large content library built in one day.

Compare packages
How do I get started?

Send us a message through the contact page, or email us, with what you have in mind and the package or service you’re interested in. We’ll get back to you within one business day to talk through your idea and lock in a time to shoot.

Get in touch
What happens once we’re working together?

Before the shoot we meet to plan how your video, or each video, will look, including the storyline. On the day we run through everything once more, then shoot. Afterwards we edit and deliver your content within 1–5 days, with one revision session included.

See our process
How quickly will I get my content?

Between 1 and 5 days, depending on your package. Starter Pack: 1 day delivery. Content Drop: 1-2 day delivery. Creator Pack: 3 day delivery. Brand Day Package: delivered within 3-5 days, guaranteed.

Are revisions included?

Yes. Every package includes one revision session. For the Brand Day Package, your revision round is done over a call that we schedule with you.

Where are you based? Do you travel?

We’re based in Brisbane, Queensland, and have been since 2022. We shoot across Brisbane and South East Queensland, and travel when the project calls for it.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

All kinds: cafés, trades, gyms, salons, startups, retail, real estate and more, as well as creators and personal brands.
